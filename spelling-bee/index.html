Meet your guide

Hi, I’m Coach Reed 👋

Welcome to the MathCloud Spelling Enhancer. I am Coach Reed. I will say a word, and you type it on the on-screen keypad, so no spell checker can help you. You will never see the word while you work. If you need help, press Definition, Use in a sentence, or Origin, and I will read you the clue. Origin tells you which language the word comes from. Every test has 20 words: choose one register, mix all registers, or let me surprise you with a random one. I avoid repeating words you practised recently. Afterwards I will show you the correct spelling and walk you through every word you missed. No passcode yet? Try the free demo.

  • 🗂️ Word registers by topic
