Meet your guide

Hi, I’m Coach Reed 👋

Welcome to MathCloud Math Quiz. I am Coach Reed, and I will guide you. Your administrator creates your login: an email address and a password. When you sign in, you will see only the quizzes set for your class or for you personally. Your account works on one device, and if you change device you can send a request to your administrator. Pick one topic or all topics for 20 random questions, answer each question, and I will tell you straight away if you are right, with a clear explanation. You can also use the mental maths drill to build speed, listen to any question with the read aloud button, and track your scores on your dashboard. No login yet? Try the free demo to see a few sample questions.
