Microphone tips
  • Use Chrome or Edge and allow microphone access.

  • Keep going if you make a mistake. The report will show words to practise.

How it works

Four steps from passcode to progress. Press the button and Coach Reed will read the guide to you.

  1. Sign inUse the email and password from your school, or the free demo passcode.
  2. Pick a passage and listen firstHear it read aloud while each word lights up.
  3. Start reading aloudAllow the microphone and read at your natural pace, then finish.
  4. Review and practiseSee your WPM and accuracy, then say each word to your coach until it is right.

Good to know: the page stays fully active while the voice is reading. Press Stop voice or tap any button whenever you like.

One quick step

Turn on your microphone

Your reading coach needs to hear you. Turn it on once and it will stay ready for every passage and every word.

  1. Click the lock or settings icon next to the web address.
  2. Set Microphone to Allow.
  3. Come back here and press Try again.
